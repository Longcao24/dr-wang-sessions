REASON FOR VISIT
Low back pain

SUBJECTIVE
A 54-year-old female with morbid obesity, chronic pain with opioid dependence and opioid-related anxiety on Suboxone presents with an acute exacerbation of low back pain. Right-sided low back pain began 3 days before this visit after attempting to carry her mother. Pain severity is 8/10 and is worsened by standing and sitting.

Since the last visit, she has continued Suboxone 8-2 mg BID and has not had obvious opioid withdrawal. Ozempic was discontinued. Metformin 500 mg BID was started. She was encouraged to continue clonidine for anxiety and to continue vitamin D supplementation. She is reluctant to start an SSRI for anxiety.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: Morbid obesity
HEENT: Pupils without abnormal dilation
Abdominal: Abdominal distention, obesity
Musculoskeletal: Tenderness over bilateral sacroiliac joint regions right greater than left, pelvic crest height discrepancy approximately 1.5 inches with left higher than right, Patrick test positive, straight leg raise negative bilaterally
Integumentary: No rash, no abnormal sweating, no eczema over ankles
Neurological: Patellar reflexes 1+ bilaterally, no focal motor deficits

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
54-year-old female with morbid obesity and chronic pain with opioid dependence on Suboxone presenting with acute right-sided low back pain after lifting, with exam notable for sacroiliac region tenderness right greater than left, pelvic asymmetry, and positive Patrick test, most consistent with acute sacroiliac joint sprain on chronic sacroiliac joint disease/instability.

1. SACROILIAC JOINT SPRAIN
Acute exacerbation after lifting with sacroiliac region tenderness and positive Patrick test; pain improved after in-office treatment
 -Perform manipulation/stretching and deep muscle massage of bilateral shoulder, mid back, and lower back muscles in office
 -Recommend routine home exercise program
 -Clear to return to work

2. OPIOID DEPENDENCE
On Suboxone for opioid withdrawal prevention and pain control; no obvious withdrawal reported
 -Continue Suboxone 8-2 mg BID
 -Encourage continued clonidine for anxiety and opioid-related symptoms

3. HYPERGLYCEMIA
Addressed with medication initiation
 -Start metformin 500 mg BID
 -Provide diet counseling

4. MORBID OBESITY
Ongoing; recent weight increase noted since last visit
 -Discontinue Ozempic due to unavailability
 -Encourage continued diet control efforts

5. ANXIETY
Opioid-related anxiety and mood disturbance; reluctant to start SSRI
 -Provide education and counseling
 -Refer to psychiatry as needed

Follow-up/Disposition:
Return to work with routine home exercise program agreed upon by patient
