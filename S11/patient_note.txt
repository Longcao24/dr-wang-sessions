REASON FOR VISIT
Alcohol relapse

SUBJECTIVE
59-year-old male with history of chronic pain and alcohol dependence presents for help quitting alcohol after relapse. He previously responded well to non-pharmacologic pain management. Valium was last prescribed June 9, about four months before this visit. He describes social and psychological trauma and reports developing a social relationship with his therapist, after which he briefly stayed on a psychiatric observation unit. After discharge home, he restarted alcohol use; last alcohol use was yesterday. He recognizes restarting alcohol was not appropriate and wants to completely stop alcohol again.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: No acute distress
HEENT: No signs of trauma, pupils without abnormality
Neck: No rigidity
Respiratory: No stridor
Abdominal: Moderately distended, obesity
Musculoskeletal: No edema
Neurological: No obvious focal deficits, no tremor of hands, alert and oriented, speech fluent and coherent

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
59-year-old male with alcohol dependence and recent relapse with last alcohol use yesterday, currently without tremor or focal neurologic deficits on exam, presenting requesting assistance with alcohol cessation and withdrawal prevention and needing connection to ongoing outpatient care.

1. ALCOHOL DEPENDENCE
Recent relapse with last alcohol use yesterday; seeking alcohol cessation support and withdrawal prevention
 -Provide education and counseling
 -Prescribe Valium for 1 month
 -Recommend psychiatry consultation
 -Recommend primary care or family medicine follow-up for ongoing alcohol use disorder management and medication wean planning if abstinent
 -Discuss nutrition
 -Continue vitamin supplementation with vitamin B complex, vitamin B12, vitamin B1

Follow-up/Disposition:
-Follow up with primary care physician and seek psychiatry consultation
