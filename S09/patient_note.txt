REASON FOR VISIT
Low back pain follow up

SUBJECTIVE
A 42-year-old female with a history of chronic low back pain and morbid obesity presents for follow-up of low back pain after completing her 12th session of physical therapy treatment. Low back pain persists with the same pattern and has been worse for the past few days. Pain is constant with intermittent worsening and is aggravated by prolonged sitting. Pain occasionally radiates from the low back to the thigh.

She has been taking oxcarbazepine as prescribed most of the time and notes it has been helpful for pain control, though pain persists. After starting metformin 500 mg once daily, she has had nausea and increased bowel movements about 3 to 4 times per day. She notes a 3 lb weight gain since the last visit.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: No acute distress, morbid obesity
HEENT: No facial weakness, no abnormal discharge from ears or nose
Neck: No rigidity
Musculoskeletal: Tenderness of lower back paraspinal muscles, tenderness of bilateral sacroiliac joint region right greater than left, Patrick test positive
Abdominal: Distended with obesity, soft
Neurological: Alert and oriented, speech fluent and coherent, no focal motor or sensory deficits

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
42-year-old female with chronic low back pain and morbid obesity with persistent low back pain with intermittent radiation to the thigh, exam notable for paraspinal and sacroiliac region tenderness right greater than left and positive Patrick test, consistent with sacroiliac joint dysfunction with sciatic nerve irritation, with GI side effects since starting metformin.

1. SACROILIAC JOINT DYSFUNCTION
Persistent low back pain with intermittent radiation to thigh, exam with sacroiliac region tenderness right greater than left and positive Patrick test; pelvic crest height discrepancy about 1.5 inches left greater than right noted today and resolved after manual treatment
- Perform manual manipulation and stretching of bilateral sacroiliac joints and low back in clinic today
- Demonstrate home management techniques and home exercise program for sacroiliac joint pain rehabilitation

2. PREDIABETES
On metformin with planned dose escalation for glycemic control and weight reduction
- Increase metformin from 500 mg once daily to 500 mg twice daily

3. MORBID OBESITY
Weight gain of 3 lb since last visit
- Counsel on diet control and routine home exercise
- Instruct to keep a diet diary
- Review food content and eating frequency for weight reduction

4. VITAMIN D INSUFFICIENCY
Vitamin D supplementation addressed
- Continue vitamin D supplementation

5. NEUROPATHIC PAIN
Partial benefit from oxcarbazepine with inconsistent use
- Counsel on oxcarbazepine effects and encourage taking medication on time

Follow-up/Disposition:
Follow up in 2 months to reevaluate treatment response and adjust medications accordingly
