REASON FOR VISIT
Weight and pain follow up

SUBJECTIVE
50-year-old female with morbid obesity, chronic low back pain, bipolar disorder managed by psychiatry, and diabetes on metformin presenting for weight reduction and pain management follow-up. Low back pain has been constant but more tolerable. Weight has increased since the last visit without an identified reason. Mood has been essentially stable with obvious improvement compared with prior, and she has not had new anxiety. No headache. She has been keeping herself away from opioid medications.

Since the last visit, she restarted Topamax from a prior prescription and is tolerating it well.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: Morbid obesity, no acute distress, appropriate to environment
HEENT: No tearing, no swelling, no facial weakness
Neck: No rigidity, no JVD
Abdominal: Distended
Musculoskeletal: No chest wall deformity, increased adiposity of bilateral extremities
Integumentary: No edema
Neurological: Bilateral calves soft
Psychiatric: Mood improved

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
50-year-old female with morbid obesity, chronic low back pain, bipolar disorder, and diabetes with interval weight gain, persistent but improved-tolerability low back pain, stable/improved mood, and ongoing avoidance of opioids, presenting for weight reduction and pain management follow-up.

1. MORBID OBESITY
Ongoing severe obesity with continued weight gain since last visit
 -Start Ozempic 0.25 once weekly for 1 month
 -Encourage home exercise and diet control
 -Encourage diet diary, symptom record, and tracking of exercise intensity and amount

2. CHRONIC LOW BACK PAIN
Constant low back pain with improved tolerability; avoiding opioids
 -Educate to continue avoiding opioid medications
 -Continue naltrexone
 -Renew gabapentin at current low dose

3. DIABETES MELLITUS
On metformin; management discussed in context of weight reduction and glycemic control
 -Renew metformin 850-150 mg

4. BIPOLAR DISORDER
Mood stable and improved; continues psychiatric care
 -Continue lamotrigine at chronic dose
 -Discontinue Topamax 25 mg TID
 -Restart Topamax 50 mg BID

5. ANXIETY SYMPTOMS
No new anxiety reported; clonidine discussed for opioid-related anxiety and pain control
 -Continue clonidine at chronic dose

Follow-up/Disposition:
- Follow up in 1 month to reassess treatment response and adjust medications accordingly
