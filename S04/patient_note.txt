REASON FOR VISIT
Pain management

SUBJECTIVE
A 63-year-old female with a history of lupus and morbid obesity presents for pain management and weight reduction follow-up. Since the last visit, she has been performing the home exercise program routinely. Mid back and lower back pain have dramatically decreased, with current pain severity 3 to 4 out of 10 and tolerable. Sleep has improved. Her husband is present and has been helping with home massage and home exercises, which she feels has been beneficial. She has had gradual weight loss and is down about 2 lb since the last visit. She feels more energetic and is pleased with her improvement.

Metformin was increased from 500 mg once daily to 500 mg twice daily today.
