CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Abdominal pain

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
A 57-year-old female with liver cirrhosis with ascites, history of multiple abdominal operations, chronic abdominal pain, and chronic back pain presents for ongoing abdominal pain management. She has been on opioid pain medications for more than 8 years and had been on the same dose of hydromorphone for the past few years, but it was not fully controlling pain and she self-increased the dose. Since the last visit, hydromorphone was discontinued and buprenorphine 2 mg four times a day was started. She has not taken any hydromorphone since then and feels happy she successfully discontinued it, with no withdrawal symptoms and no obvious pain exacerbation. Abdominal pain persists. She feels nervous/anxious about possible pain exacerbation. Escitalopram was prescribed but she has not started it yet. Sleep has been better with slightly improved pain control. She continues to have long-term nausea without vomiting. No signs of GI bleeding. No new constipation. No new injury.
