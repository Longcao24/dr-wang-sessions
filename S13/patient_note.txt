REASON FOR VISIT
Chronic pain follow up

SUBJECTIVE
A 62-year-old female with chronic pain related to hip degenerative arthritis and necrosis and chronic pain related anxiety presents for follow-up. Since the last visit, mood, mobility, and pain tolerance have significantly improved. Weight has gradually decreased, and pain is more tolerable. She states she no longer has severe anxiety. She feels she is on the right track and is interested in medication adjustment.

She reports alcohol use and agreed to quit alcohol except for social drinking on Chinese holidays. She has been using marijuana for pain control.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: No acute distress, ambulating with a rolling walker
Neck: No rigidity
Abdominal: Soft, benign
Musculoskeletal: Tenderness over left hip, decreased left hip active range of motion, decreased tenderness over mid-back and lower back compared with prior exam, chest wall without deformity, extremities without edema
Neurological: Alert and oriented, fluent coherent speech, no new motor deficits
Psychiatric: Talkative and appropriate, good mood, able to listen to differing opinions

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
62-year-old female with chronic pain syndrome in the setting of left hip degenerative arthritis and necrosis with improved pain tolerance, mobility, mood, and anxiety since last visit; exam notable for persistent left hip tenderness and decreased active ROM. Visit focused on medication reconciliation/adjustments, non-opioid pain strategy, and counseling regarding alcohol and marijuana use.
