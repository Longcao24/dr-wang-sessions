REASON FOR VISIT
Chronic pain follow up

SUBJECTIVE
A 71-year-old female with severe osteoporosis and a history of multiple fractures and prolonged opioid use for chronic post-traumatic pain presents for follow-up of ongoing pain and anxiety. Pain is constant with intermittent worsening and interferes with quality of life. She has a history of left elbow fracture and right hip fracture, with recent right hip hardware removal and replacement. Hip pain has subsided compared with prior, but she still has significant pain. No new injury recently. She continues to use a wheelchair, particularly for outdoor activity.

Since the last visit, escitalopram has decreased anxiety and nervousness and she is sleeping better, with partial improvement in pain. Despite this, she continues to require opioid pain medications for pain control. She has been on hydrocodone for 1 year.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
Musculoskeletal: Left elbow tenderness with mild local erythema, allodynia to light touch, right hip tenderness without local erythema, warmth, or fluid collection, functional limb strength with range of motion present but limited by pain during strength testing
HEENT: No swelling, no facial weakness, no drainage from ears or nose
Neck: No rigidity
Respiratory: No respiratory distress
Abdominal: Benign, soft
Cardiac: No chest wall deformity

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
71-year-old female with severe osteoporosis and multiple prior fractures including left elbow and right hip fracture status post right hip hardware removal and replacement, presenting with persistent chronic post-traumatic pain on long-term opioid therapy with associated anxiety symptoms improved on escitalopram. Exam notable for left elbow and right hip tenderness with allodynia at the elbow and no signs of local infection at the hip.

1. CHRONIC PAIN
Chronic post-traumatic pain with persistent functional impact and focal tenderness at left elbow and right hip
-द Discuss desensitization massage
-Discuss temporary local modalities
-Encourage muscle strengthening and active range of motion exercises

2. LONG TERM OPIOID THERAPY
Prolonged opioid use for approximately 1 year; no new fracture or recent surgery discussed as indication for short-acting opioids
-Review New York State PMP
-Provide counseling on opioid effects and side effects and endorphin balance
-Discontinue oxycodone
-Start methadone 0.5 mg twice a day for 2 weeks, then change to 0.5 mg once a day with gradual taper off
-Start clonidine patch

3. ANXIETY DISORDER
Improved anxiety, nervousness, and sleep on escitalopram, with partial pain benefit
-Continue escitalopram 20 mg half tablet twice a day
-Review medication effects and side effects

4. OSTEOPOROSIS
Severe osteoporosis on treatment with history of multiple fractures
-Reinforce that fractures are not new and have been treated

Follow-up/Disposition:
-Follow up in 1 month to reevaluate treatment response and adjust medications accordingly
-Call for new pain exacerbation
