REASON FOR VISIT
Right shoulder pain follow up

SUBJECTIVE
69-year-old female with history of morbid obesity and borderline diabetes presents with her husband for pain management follow-up for right shoulder pain. Her husband accompanied her to the visit.

Right shoulder pain has persisted in the same constant pattern with intermittent worsening and is significantly interfering with quality of life. No new injury. She has been performing home exercises as instructed for shoulder active and passive range of motion, but pain persists. Since starting Ozempic, she has had increased diarrhea and nausea and is reluctant to continue it. Constipation occurs intermittently.

OBJECTIVE
PHYSICAL EXAM
General Appearance: No acute distress
Neck: No neck rigidity
Abdominal: Soft
Musculoskeletal: Right shoulder active range of motion limited about 30 percent at end-range abduction and extension, right shoulder crepitus with range of motion, rotator cuff challenge testing positive for pain, right-sided shoulder region tenderness greater than left
Neurological: No facial weakness
Psychiatric: Appropriate to environment

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
69-year-old female with morbid obesity and borderline diabetes presenting for persistent right shoulder pain despite home ROM exercises, with limited ROM, crepitus, and pain with rotator cuff provocative testing, also with GI side effects since starting Ozempic and interval weight gain.

1. RIGHT SHOULDER PAIN
Persistent pain interfering with quality of life with exam notable for limited ROM, crepitus, and pain with rotator cuff provocative testing
 -Perform right shoulder steroid injection using Betadine skin prep, 0.5% Marcaine 2 cc and dexamethasone 4 mg
 -Encourage continued home active and passive range of motion exercises

2. NAUSEA DIARRHEA CONSTIPATION
GI symptoms temporally associated with starting Ozempic, including nausea and diarrhea with intermittent constipation
 -Encourage continued Ozempic
 -Start ondansetron 4 mg BID PRN
 -Give Duplex for constipation control

3. MORBID OBESITY
Interval weight gain of 10 lb compared with last visit
 -Discuss diet control and routine home exercise

4. DEPRESSED MOOD
Mood and pain tolerance noted improved on chronic dose of Myrbugine
 -Continue Myrbugine

Follow-up/Disposition:
- Follow up in 1 to 2 months to reevaluate treatment response and adjust medications accordingly
