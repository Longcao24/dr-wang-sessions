REASON FOR VISIT
Pain management follow-up

SUBJECTIVE
A 47-year-old female with severe morbid obesity, chronic back pain, pain-related mood disorder, and history of gastric bypass complicated by vitamin B12 deficiency with neuropathic pain presents for chronic pain and morbid obesity management follow-up. She participated in a weight reduction clinic and dietitian education program and started the dietitian-recommended diet for weight reduction. Weight is down 13 lb since the last visit. Mood has improved with better pain control. She has been more active with small amounts of exercise and is no longer using a cane or walker for ambulation.

She has not taken a vitamin B12 injection for the last 1 month. She has a pending abdominal surgery.

OBJECTIVE
Physical Exam:
General Appearance: No acute distress
HEENT: No tearing, no facial weakness
Neck: No rigidity
Respiratory: No chest deformity, no respiratory distress
Abdominal: Soft, obese
Musculoskeletal: Bilateral calves soft, mid-back paraspinal muscle and joint tenderness with decreased intensity compared with prior exam
Neurological: No head tremor
Psychiatric: Appropriate to environment, spontaneous smiling, no signs of anxiety

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
47-year-old female with morbid obesity status post gastric bypass complicated by vitamin B12 deficiency with neuropathic pain, chronic back pain, and pain-related mood disorder, presenting for follow-up after participating in a weight reduction program with reported 13 lb weight loss, improved mood, improved pain control, and decreased paraspinal tenderness on exam.

1. MORBID OBESITY
Improving with weight reduction clinic participation and 13 lb weight loss since last visit
- Continue weight reduction program

2. CHRONIC BACK PAIN
Improving with reported better pain control and decreased mid-back paraspinal tenderness compared with prior exam
- Continue current chronic pain treatment strategy
- Encourage home exercise and increased walking in the home and community
- Counsel on safety and fall prevention

3. PERIPHERAL NEUROPATHY
Neuropathic pain pattern improving on current regimen
- Continue oxcarbazepine 300 mg BID
- Encourage self-massage/desensitization of distal limbs for tingling, numbness, and burning pain

4. VITAMIN B12 DEFICIENCY
History of gastric bypass with vitamin B12 deficiency; missed injections for 1 month
- Administer vitamin B12 1000 mcg IM in the right deltoid today

5. PAIN RELATED MOOD DISORDER
Improved mood with current regimen; no side effects reported
- Renew current dose of Wellbutrin in combination with naltrexone
- Continue mood stabilizer prescribed by primary care physician

6. UPCOMING ABDOMINAL SURGERY
Perioperative pain medication planning discussed in setting of naltrexone use
- Hold naltrexone 1 week prior to surgery
- Use surgeon-prescribed postoperative pain medications after surgery
- Review postoperative treatment response and adjust medications after surgery

Follow-up/Disposition:
- Follow up 1 month after surgery to review treatment response and adjust medication regimen
